Tightest budget

MSI Modern 15 — cheapest at ฿14,990, genuine Office, RAM upgradable to 64GB. Check stock first.

MacBook Neo — 15.5 hrs battery, 1.22 kg, Liquid Retina display. Premium build, long lifespan.

Apple ecosystem

MacBook Neo — if the family already uses iPhone/iPad, seamless integration + 5+ year support + great resale value.
